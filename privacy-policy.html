Privacy Policy

Official B2B data protection policy and privacy practices for Blizz UAE.

1. Introduction & Scope

Blizz UAE ("we", "us", "our", "Seller") is committed to protecting the privacy and corporate data of our wholesale customers, distributors, and online visitors ("you", "your"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you interact with our website, request bulk quotations, or register B2B enquiries.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you submit our wholesale enquiry forms or contact us regarding corporate supplies. This includes:

  • Full Name and Job Title
  • Company Name and Corporate Address
  • Corporate Email Address and Phone Number
  • Bulk product specifications, monthly cleaning detergent volume estimates, and purchase preferences

3. How We Use Your Data

Your business information is strictly utilized to process B2B requests and fulfill bulk orders. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Generate wholesale quotations and manage logistics/dispatches across Dubai and Jebel Ali
  • Send email updates regarding your submitted leads and corporate account statistics
  • Comply with financial regulations, audit reviews, and corporate tax compliance in the UAE

4. Data Storage and Security

All client lead details and wholesale enquiry records are stored securely in our MongoDB database system. We implement robust physical, administrative, and technological safety measures to defend your information from unauthorized access, modification, or exposure.

5. Information Sharing & Third-Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your company's data to third-party marketing entities. We only share essential details (e.g. company address and phone number) with verified logistics partners, Jebel Ali delivery hubs, or custom brokers solely as required to facilitate the delivery of your chemical orders.

6. Your Corporate Rights

You can request to view, edit, or permanently erase your corporate contact records from our database system at any time. To make a request, please contact our administrative desk directly at Blizzbusinessinfo@gmail.com.
