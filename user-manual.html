B2B Product User Manual & Safety Guide

Comprehensive usage instructions, chemical dilution standards, safety precautions, and storage guidelines for facility operators across the UAE.

1. Purpose & Overview

This B2B Product User Manual serves as standard operating guidelines for facility operators, housekeeping staff, corporate janitorial teams, and healthcare compliance managers using Blizz UAE commercial cleaning chemicals. Adhering to these guidelines ensures optimal hygienic performance, chemical efficiency, and workplace safety.

2. Commercial Dilution & Application Matrix

Blizz chemical formulas are highly concentrated to reduce packaging waste and lower bulk logistics costs. Follow the standard dilution ratios below for general use:

Product Line Application Recommended Ratio Contact Time
Commercial Laundry Liquid Automated Industrial Washers 40 - 60 ml per 10kg load Full wash cycle
Fabric Softener Final Rinse Cycle 25 - 35 ml per 10kg load 3 - 5 minutes
Hospital Disinfectant High-Touch Hard Surfaces 1 : 20 (Water dilution) 10 minutes minimum
Floor Cleaner (Lavender) Tile, Marble, & Terrazzo 1 : 50 (Mop & bucket) Air dry
Glass & Window Cleaner Mirrors & Glazed Panels Ready-To-Use Spray Wipe immediately

3. Safety Precautions & Handling

  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Cleaning personnel must wear chemical-resistant rubber gloves and protective eye goggles when dispensing concentrated formulations.
  • Ventilation: Ensure adequate room airflow when spraying surface sanitizers in enclosed facilities.
  • First Aid Measures: In case of direct eye contact, rinse immediately with clean running water for at least 15 minutes and seek medical evaluation. If ingested, do not induce vomiting; contact emergency emergency services or call UAE local health authorities (+971 800 342).

4. Storage & Shelf Life Requirements

Store all Blizz canisters (20L, 5L, and 1L) in a cool, dry chemical storage area away from direct UAE sunlight. Maintain storage temperatures between 15°C and 35°C. Keep canisters tightly closed when not in use. Sealed products carry a standard shelf life of 24 months from the manufacturing date stamped on the container.

5. SDS & Environmental Compliance

Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for all 50+ Blizz product formulas are available upon request for corporate compliance audits. All surfactants utilized in Blizz chemical formulations comply with UAE municipal environmental standards for biodegradability and effluent discharge.
